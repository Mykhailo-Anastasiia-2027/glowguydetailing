Questions

Before you book

How much does detailing cost?

Interior Reset is $205 for a sedan, $230 SUV/truck, $255 for 3-row/large. Full Glow is $280–$360 by size, Winter Salt Rescue $155–$205. Use the price calculator at the top of this page — that's your real number, confirmed in writing before we start.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes — we're fully mobile across the Niagara Region, Hamilton and GTA. Driveway, garage, underground condo parking or office lot. In winter, interior work and salt removal happen in garages and underground parking, so we run all year.

How long does a detail take?

Interior Reset: about 2–3 hours. Full Glow: 3–4 hours. Showroom + Sealant: 5–6 hours. Salt Rescue: 1.5–2 hours. We'll confirm timing with your quote based on your car's size and condition.

Can you get winter salt out of carpets?

That's exactly what the Winter Salt Rescue is for. Hot-water extraction pulls dried salt out of carpets and mats before it destroys the fibres. Fresh salt comes out fully; older stains improve dramatically.

How do I book?

Call or text 437-551-5149 or message us on Instagram or Facebook. Tell us your vehicle, get one fixed price in writing, pick your day — done.
