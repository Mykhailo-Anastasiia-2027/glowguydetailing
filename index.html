Questions

Before you book

How much does detailing cost?

Interior Reset is $150 for a sedan, $175 SUV/truck, $200 for 3-row/large. Full Glow is $230–$310 by size, Winter Salt Rescue $99–$139. Polishing starts at $250, ceramic at $450. Use the price calculator at the top of this page — that's your real number, confirmed in writing from your photos.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes — we're fully mobile across the Niagara Region and Hamilton. Driveway, garage, underground condo parking or office lot. In winter, interior work and salt removal happen in garages and underground parking, so we run all year.

How long does a detail take?

Interior Reset: about 2–3 hours. Full Glow: 3–4 hours. Showroom + Sealant: 5–6 hours. Salt Rescue: 1.5–2 hours. We'll confirm timing with your quote based on your car's size and condition.

Can you get winter salt out of carpets?

That's exactly what the Winter Salt Rescue is for. Hot-water extraction pulls dried salt out of carpets and mats before it destroys the fibres. Fresh salt comes out fully; older stains improve dramatically.
